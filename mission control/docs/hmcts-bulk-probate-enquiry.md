# HMCTS Bulk Probate Data — Enquiry Draft

**To:** HMCTS Disclosure Team (bulk data access)
**Email:** `data.access@justice.gov.uk`
**From:** 9amLeads
**Subject:** Enquiry — bulk access to probate grant records (England & Wales)

---

Dear HMCTS Probate Records Team,

I am writing to enquire about bulk access to probate grant records issued in England and Wales, for the purpose of building a commercial probate-lead data service (9amLeads).

I understand that HMCTS operates a bulk-access service for wills and probate records, and that a new probate record normally becomes publicly searchable approximately 14 days after the grant is issued. I would like to explore a commercial data arrangement.

**Questions I would be grateful to have answered:**

1. **Daily electronic files** — Is it possible to receive a daily electronic feed (or weekly/monthly file) of newly issued probate grants? What is the data format (CSV / XML / other) and delivery method (SFTP / API)?

2. **Record fields** — For each grant, does the data include:
   - Deceased's full name
   - Deceased's last known **address** (street, town, postcode)? This is the most important field for our service.
   - Executor / personal representative details
   - Estate value (if published)
   - Grant issue date
   - Grant type (probate / letters of administration)
   - Whether the deceased address is the full registered address or a redacted/partial version?

3. **Cost** — What is the pricing model? Per-record cost, or a flat monthly/annual fee? Is there a minimum commitment or a free tier for evaluation?

4. **Commercial redistribution** — Is the data licensed for commercial use, specifically for the purpose of providing **derived lead records** (e.g. "a grant was issued for an estate at this address, handled by this executor") to our business subscribers (probate solicitors, house-clearance companies, probate-property buyers, removal firms, etc.)? What are the permitted and prohibited uses?

5. **Latency** — Confirming the approximate time between a grant being issued and it appearing in the bulk feed (I understand the public search shows records ~14 days after issue — is the bulk feed faster, slower, or the same?).

6. **Coverage** — Does the feed cover England and Wales only? Are separate arrangements needed for Scotland (Sheriff Courts) and Northern Ireland?

7. **Agreement process** — What is the application process, expected timeline, and any compliance/security requirements (e.g. data protection agreement, intended-use declaration)?

Please let me know the correct contact and process for submitting a formal request. If there is a standard data-access agreement or application form, I would be happy to complete it.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
**9amLeads**
