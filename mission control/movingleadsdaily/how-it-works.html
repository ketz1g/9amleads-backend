How Moving Leads Daily Works

Three simple steps to start receiving daily moving leads. No setup fees, no contracts, no minimum commitment.

1

Tell Us Your Area

Enter the postcodes, towns, or cities you want to target. We monitor every property that goes SSTC (Sold Subject to Contract) in your chosen areas. We'll show you estimated weekly lead volume before you commit.

Example: SW1, SW3, SW5 — or London, Manchester, Birmingham

2

We Scan Overnight

Every morning at 6am our system scans Rightmove, Zoopla, and OnTheMarket for newly listed SSTC properties matching your criteria. We filter by property type, number of bedrooms, and price range — so you only get relevant leads.

Data sources: Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket, Land Registry

3

Your Lead Sheet Arrives at 9am

By 9am every morning your fresh lead sheet is waiting in your email inbox. Open it, pick up the phone, and start calling. Each lead includes the property address, number of bedrooms, listing date, and estimated move window. No dashboard to check, no app to open — just leads ready when you start work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact homeowners without a name?

You don't always need a full name. The estate agent handling the property can often make an introduction, or you can reach out via social media. The key advantage is timing — being the first removal company to contact them before they've even started looking.

What if nobody responds to my outreach?

Our customers see strong results from being first to reach out. One booking typically pays for 2-6 months of subscription. Plus, you have a 90-day money-back guarantee — if you don't book 3+ jobs, you get a full refund.

What time do leads arrive?

Your fresh lead sheet lands in your email inbox every morning by 9am, including weekends. Our system scans property portals at 6am, processes and filters the data, and delivers your personalised lead sheet before you start work. Same time, every day — so you can build it into your morning routine.

Can I try before I commit?

Yes. Start with a 7-day free trial. No card required. You'll receive daily leads for a full week. If it's not for you, cancel and keep everything you received.

How is this different from MoverAlerts?

Moving Leads Daily is half the price of MoverAlerts, offers instant self-service signup (no phone call required), filters by property type/bedrooms/price, and gives you a 7-day free trial. Our data is scraped live daily — theirs comes from a periodically updated database.
