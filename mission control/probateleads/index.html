Questions? We Have Answers.

A probate grant is a legal document issued by the Probate Registry that confirms an executor's authority to administer a deceased person's estate. Before executors can sell property, close bank accounts, or distribute assets, they must apply for probate — and every new grant on the official register is a genuine, time-sensitive instruction for you.

We check the official probate register daily and capture new grants within 24-48 hours of publication. Your digest arrives every morning at 9am, giving you a clear head start over competitors who rely on referrals or word of mouth.

We cover every county in England, Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. Whether you practise locally or nationally, you choose exactly which areas to monitor — so you only ever receive leads relevant to your practice.

Each lead includes the deceased's name, date of grant, registry location, grant type, and estimated estate value where available — presented in a clean, easy-to-scan email that arrives at 9am every morning.
