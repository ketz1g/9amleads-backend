Auto Print & Post

Your new leads are printed and posted automatically, every day at 9am
Auto Print & Post OFF
How it works. You don't have to lift a finger:
  1. Your new leads arrive in your inbox every morning at 9am.
  2. We automatically print & post your materials to those leads, using your saved leaflet/letter and cover letter.
  3. You're charged automatically for exactly what was sent (e.g. 12 letters × £1.49 = £17.88), from the card saved on your account.
  4. You get a confirmation email each time a batch is sent, and every lead is marked "Posted" in your dashboard.
  5. Choose "Send once", or "Send now + 2 weeks + 4 weeks". For repeat, the same leads are automatically mailed again at 2 weeks and 4 weeks (already paid for), each with its own confirmation email.
You control it with a daily spend limit, a monthly cap, and you can switch it off anytime. No mailing = no charge.

How often each lead is mailed
Auto Print & Post sends each new lead once, on the day it arrives at 9am. No repeats, no scheduling to manage.
Follow-ups. you choose when
  • Send a follow-up any time: after a lead has been mailed, it stays on your My Leads page with a "Post Again" button. Click it to send them another letter or flyer whenever you want. 2 weeks, a month, or any date you choose.
  • Bulk follow-ups: on My Leads, use Select All on the week or month view, then Print & Post to re-mail all of them in one go.
  • Repeat on send: when sending a lead, you can optionally pick "now + 2 weeks + 1 month" to pre-schedule follow-ups automatically.
Why following up matters: Most conversions don't happen on the first contact. Following up a lead a week or two later. whether by re-sending a letter, dropping in person, or making a phone call. dramatically increases your chances of winning the work. One follow-up can be the difference between "we found someone else" and a signed job.
Each follow-up mailing is charged separately when you send it (letter £1.49 / leaflet £1.99 / leaflet + letter £2.99 each).
Save a card for automatic billing
Auto Print & Post charges your card automatically each day it sends leads (e.g. 12 letters × £1.49 = £17.88). We'll only charge when leads are actually mailed, up to your daily spend limit.
How to save it:
  1. Click "Save Card Securely" below.
  2. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe page to enter your card (we never see your card number).
  3. This only stores your card for future auto-billing. No charge is made now.
  4. You'll be brought back here and Auto Print & Post billing will be ready.
