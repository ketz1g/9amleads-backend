Getting your leaflet right

We print your leaflet double-sided, so always send a front AND a back. Here is what to put on each side to get the phone ringing.

🎯

Front — grab their attention

One clear headline and your best offer. Big, bold text they can read at a glance, plus a strong photo of your work or team. Give them a reason to turn it over.

📞

Back — make it easy to call

Your phone number, website, email and opening hours. Add your reviews, accreditations or a QR code. This is where people find how to reach you.

Why both sides? Double-sided printing costs the same, so you get twice the space at no extra cost. The front wins their attention; the back turns it into a call. A blurry or cluttered leaflet gets binned — keep it clean, high-contrast and easy to read.
How to do it: 1) Download a blank template or use the templates above. 2) Design your front and back. 3) Upload both in Print & Post and switch on Auto Send — we print, address and post them for you.
