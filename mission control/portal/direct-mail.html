Guides & Top tips
How to use Edit / Position
Click Edit / Position on your saved flyer to open the editor:
• Keep everything inside the BLUE dashed lines — that's the safe print area
• Drag the flyer to move it around the page
• Drag any blue square (handle) to stretch it up/down or left/right
• Use + / − / scroll wheel to scale the whole design
• On the Back, don't drag your design into the white address zone shown on the preview — it stays clean white for the recipient's address
• Click Save Positioned Flyer when it looks right
The print matches exactly what you position. you're in full control.
Why the white space?
Royal Mail machines read the recipient's address automatically. To do that reliably, the address must sit on a clean white area with no design, images or text underneath. If anything overlaps it, the address can't be read and the flyer may be delayed or returned.
The easy way (recommended)
You don't need to change anything. your print-ready version above already has the white space added. Just download it and use it.
Want your own design?
Download the design template below (it shows the exact bleed, safe zone and the clear zone marked). Design within the safe zone and leave the bottom ~28% (the orange clear zone) blank. then upload your finished front and back.
Top tips
• Keep logos, contact details and your call to action inside the safe zone
• Make the background reach the edge (bleed) so there are no white borders
• Use 300 DPI images for crisp print (we auto-upscale for you)
• Avoid text across the bottom clear zone
• After editing, hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5) if the preview looks old

Design Template (Print Specs)

Download the official blank template for your chosen format. it shows the exact bleed margin (red), safe zone (blue) and address clear zone (orange) so your design prints perfectly. Front and back templates included.

Design on the template, then upload your front and back below. We print edge-to-edge (300 DPI) and keep the address clear zone white for Royal Mail.

Each region's postal provider (e.g. Royal Mail) has strict requirements on where the address sits so your mail can be machine-read, sorted and delivered correctly. We print the address and the postage indicia stamp directly onto your item. The template markings show you the exact borders to design within. they are guides only and never printed on the final item.

1
Trim Marks (red line)
We print on larger paper and then cut it down to size. The trim marks are where we cut. Anything outside them is removed.
2
Bleed Margin
The area aligned with the trim marks that gets cut off. Make your background colour / design overflow into this margin so you get an edge-to-edge print with no white edges. ✓ We do this automatically. your design is upscaled so it fills the full sheet.
3
Safe Zone (blue line)
Keep all important text, logos, QR codes, contact details and your call to action inside this area. Printers can print with a minor skew, so anything outside the safe zone risks being cut off. Recommended: everything within the Safe Zone.
4
Clear Zone (orange area)
The recipient address and barcode are printed here and must be machine-readable. A white overlay is applied by default so nothing disturbs reading. Do not put dark colours, images or markings across this area. it can incur a hand-sorting charge or leave your item undeliverable. We keep this zone white for you automatically.
Important: If the address area is not kept clear, the item may not be machine-readable, may incur a hand-sorting charge, and could be undeliverable. Address visibility is the sender's responsibility. so please design with the clear zone in mind.
Return address: On postcards and greeting cards we can also print a return address inside the clear zone. Add it in your business profile and it's printed automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions
How are my letters and leaflets sent?

Your items are printed, folded and posted by a trusted third-party mailing service, and delivered through Royal Mail. You don't need to do anything. Once you pay, everything is handled for you.

How do I know my items were actually sent?

Every mailing shows a live tracking timeline (Received → Review → Paid → Printing → Delivered). Once dispatched, you can open a Proof of Posting PDF from the mailing's tracking view, which confirms it was printed and handed to Royal Mail.

How long does delivery take?

Items are usually printed and dispatched within 1-2 working days, then delivered by Royal Mail shortly after. The tracking timeline updates as your item moves through each stage.

Can I send to the same lead more than once?

Yes. Use the Repeat mailing option when sending to schedule automatic follow-ups (e.g. now, in 2 weeks, then a month). Each follow-up is sent automatically on its due date.
