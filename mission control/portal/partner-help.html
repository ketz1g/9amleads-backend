Partner Help Centre

Everything you need to earn with 9amLeads: your code, how to promote, helping your members get results, commission and payments. Can't find an answer? Contact us.

Getting started

1. Share your link or code

Everything is in your dashboard. Copy your link or code and share it with your members. They get a 14-day (2-week) free trial through you.

2. Invite your members

Use the Invite your members tool to paste or upload your member list, and we send a co-branded invitation carrying your code. Track who joined.

3. Earn recurring commission

You earn £25 per active paying member, per month - recurring for as long as they stay. That's £2,500/month for every 100.

Your code & link

Choose your own code

In your dashboard, use "Customise your referral code" to pick a memorable code (3-24 letters/numbers). Your link and QR update automatically. Reserved or duplicate codes are rejected.

Co-branded page

Your link (9amleads.com/r/YOURCODE) opens a page that says "In partnership with" your organisation, with the 14-day trial offer.

Attribution

Members must sign up through your link or quote your code. Attribution lasts 30 days from the first click, so you still get the credit if they sign up later.

How to promote 9amLeads

  • Frame it as a member benefit: tell your members that, as part of their membership, they get 2 weeks of free 9amLeads - fresh moving leads delivered every morning.
  • Email your member list using the ready-made wording in your dashboard's Share kit (one click to copy).
  • Add it to your newsletter or member bulletin.
  • Post it in your member forum, WhatsApp or Slack group.
  • Include it in your new-member welcome pack.
  • Share your link on LinkedIn/Facebook and on your website's partners page.
  • Print your QR code (Share kit) for events, AGMs and trade shows.
  • Ask members to quote your code when they sign up.

Helping your members get results

Print & Post

Members upload a flyer and letter once, then we print, address and post it to each lead in one click - with live postage tracking and proof of posting.

Auto Print & Post

Members can switch on Auto Send so every new lead is printed and posted automatically each morning, hands free.

Bulk Postage

In quiet weeks, members can buy packs of never-sent archive leads and we print and post a campaign to all of them.

CRM integration

Leads can be pushed straight into a member's CRM automatically at 9am (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and more).

Tip for your members: direct mail builds over 2-4 weeks of consistent posting - tell them to keep it running rather than judging it on a handful of days.

Commission & payments

What you earn

£25 per active paying customer, per month, recurring. £2,500/month per 100 active customers. No cost, no minimum, no lock-in.

When it's paid

Commission starts when a referred member becomes a paying customer. It's approved after our standard ~30-day clearance period and paid weekly by UK bank transfer, once your payout details are verified. No minimum.

If a member cancels

Commission for that customer stops when they cancel. You keep commission already earned.

FAQs

How much do I earn, and when am I paid?

£25 per active paying customer, per month, recurring. Commission is approved after a ~30-day clearance period and paid weekly by UK bank transfer once your payout details are verified. No minimum.

What trial do my members get?

An extended 14-day (2-week) free trial - double our standard 7 days. No card is required to start.

How is a member credited to me?

They must sign up through your link or quote your code. Attribution lasts 30 days from the first click.

How will I know when someone signs up with my code?

You get an email and a dashboard notification the moment someone starts a free trial with your code or link. You get another alert when they become a paying customer, and alerts for payment failures or cancellations.

Can I choose my own code?

Yes - use "Customise your referral code" in your dashboard. Link and QR update automatically.

Do I have to manage anything?

No. We handle onboarding, support, billing and the daily delivery. You just share your link/code or use the invite tool.

Can I pause or leave?

Yes - contact us and we'll pause or close your account. Commission already earned is still paid.
