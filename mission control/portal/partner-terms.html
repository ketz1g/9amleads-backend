Partner Programme Agreement

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · 9am Leads Ltd, Company No. 17402522, 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA, United Kingdom.

This Agreement governs your participation in the 9amLeads Partner Programme (the "Programme"). By applying, being approved, or using the partner dashboard, you ("Partner") agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the Programme.

1. Definitions

  • "9amLeads", "we", "us", "our" means 9am Leads Ltd.
  • "Partner" means an association, network, sales partner or affiliate accepted into the Programme.
  • "Member" means a person or business introduced by the Partner who signs up to a 9amLeads paid service using the Partner's referral code or link.
  • "Commission" means the recurring amount payable to the Partner for Active Members as described in clause 5.
  • "Active Member" means a Member who is on a paid plan and in good standing (not cancelled, refunded or in arrears).

2. Eligibility and account

  • We may accept or decline any application at our discretion.
  • You must keep your account details accurate and keep your login secure. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • You may choose your own referral code, subject to availability and our approval. We may change or reclaim codes where needed.

3. Referral and attribution

  • A Member is credited to you where they sign up through your tracked link or quote your code.
  • Attribution lasts 30 days from the first qualifying click. Attribution is first-click-wins.
  • Commission is not payable where the Member cancels, reverses or fails to pay, or where the referral is fraudulent, incentivised improperly, or breaches clause 7.

4. Member trial

Members introduced through the Programme receive an extended 14-day (2-week) free trial. No payment is taken during the trial unless the Member chooses to add a card (in which case the plan begins automatically at the end of the trial, and they can cancel before then).

5. Commission

  • You earn £25 per Active Member, per month, recurring for as long as the Member remains Active. This equates to £2,500 per month for every 100 Active Members.
  • Commission is performance-based and payable only on Active Members. There is no cost, minimum volume or lock-in to take part.
  • Commission is earned once a Member has been a paying customer for approximately one month (roughly their 4th weekly payment). It then becomes approved after our standard clearance period of approximately 30 days, and is then paid. Nothing is earned during a Member's free trial.
  • Commission rates may be reviewed in line with our pricing with reasonable notice.

6. Payouts and tax

  • Payouts are made by UK bank transfer, weekly, once Commission has cleared and your payout details (KYC and bank details) have been verified. There is no minimum payout threshold.
  • You must provide accurate identification and bank details and keep them up to date. We may withhold payouts pending verification or investigation.
  • You are solely responsible for your own tax affairs, registrations and reporting. Payouts are gross amounts and you are responsible for any tax due on them.
  • Payouts are stated in GBP. Bank charges are the responsibility of the receiving party.

7. Marketing and compliance

  • You must comply with all applicable laws, including UK data protection (UK GDPR / PECR) and advertising rules.
  • You must not send unsolicited spam, buy contact lists without a lawful basis, or misrepresent 9amLeads or its services.
  • You must use accurate, approved descriptions. Do not promise guaranteed leads, jobs or income. All earnings are illustrative and performance-based.
  • You must not make any claim that 9amLeads is endorsed by you beyond an accurate statement of partnership, and must not misuse our trademarks past the permissions we give you.
  • Where you invite your own members, you confirm you have a lawful basis to contact them and will include unsubscribe facilities.

8. Data protection

We each act as independent data controllers for information we hold about our own contacts. Where you share member contact details with us for the purpose of sending invitations, you confirm you have the necessary consents or lawful basis. We process such data in line with our Privacy Notice.
