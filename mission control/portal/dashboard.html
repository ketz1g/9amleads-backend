Frequently Asked Questions
What time are leads delivered?
Leads are delivered every morning at 9am, Monday to Friday. Weekend scrapes are included in Monday's delivery.
How are leads matched to my area?
When you sign up, you select postcode area codes (e.g. E, NW, EN). Our system scrapes property listings daily and matches them to your selected areas. You can add or change areas anytime from your dashboard.
Can I change my lead filters?
Yes. Visit your Settings page to adjust filters such as minimum bedrooms, property type, and maximum price. Changes take effect on the next day's delivery.
What happens when my free trial ends?
After your 7-day free trial, you'll be charged £25/week for the Starter plan if you haven't cancelled. You can cancel anytime from your dashboard with no penalties. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
