Work the steps

1. Win the sign-up: the first 24 hours

Contact the moment they sign upYour dashboard Leads tab shows every customer who used your code. Message them the same day. A personal welcome makes them take the 14-day trial seriously.
Send a welcome text"Thanks for starting your 9amLeads trial! Your first leads arrive tomorrow at 9am. I'll check in to see how they look." Set the expectation that leads arrive every morning.
Never let a sign-up go coldEvery customer who started a trial but never got a follow-up is a £25 commission you walked away from. The trial is only valuable if they actually use it.

2. Nurture the 14-day trial

Day 1. "How did your first batch look?"Check in after their first 9am delivery. Seeing real leads arrive is what makes the trial feel valuable.
Day 3-4. Share one tailored tipOpen the Tools tab and send the tip that fits their lead type. For a removal company: "call within the hour, the first company to call usually wins the move." For a solicitor: "contact executors within 48 hours."
Day 7. Halfway nudge"Halfway through your free trial. Have the leads been useful? Want me to help you start converting them?"
Day 10-11. The trial is ending"Your 14-day trial finishes soon. Want to make sure you don't lose your daily leads?" This is your best conversion moment.

3. Convert at the end of the trial

Ask on day 11-13They've seen daily value by now. The best time to ask is while it's front of mind. Not after it ends.
Remind them of the deal"You got 14 days free instead of the standard 7. Keep the leads going with a plan. Most start with Starter (£25/week) and upgrade later."
Handle the price objection"One extra job usually covers a month of leads." If they're a removal company, one move covers it; a solicitor, one probate file covers it.
Offer help choosingStarter £25/wk, Pro £49/wk, Enterprise £99/wk. All lead types at the same price. Suggest based on how many leads they need.

4. Get them to their second invoice (about a month in)

Your commission = the second paid invoiceYou earn £25 around a month after they sign up, once a customer pays their second invoice. Help them through the first week of paid use and your commission clears soon after.
Check in after their first paymentA quick "How are the leads looking?" message after their first paid week helps them see value. And get them to that second invoice that earns your £25.
Fix problems fastIf they say leads aren't working: suggest a different lead type, better postcode areas, or point them to the outreach toolkit. A small fix keeps them subscribed.
Congratulate their wins"Glad the leads are working!" Referrals who feel your support stay longer. And refer more customers to you.

5. Outreach best practice

Speed beats everythingContact referrals the same day they sign up. The customer who hears from you first trusts you first.
Call, don't just textCalls convert far better than texts or emails. Use the phone scripts in Tools. They're written to get the trial started.
Best times to call businessesMid-morning (10-11am) and mid-afternoon (2-4pm), Tuesday to Thursday. Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons.
Use the toolkit end-to-endPhone script → email → SMS → follow-up plan. One tool alone works; the full sequence converts.

6. The value message. Say these words

The double-trial pitch"Normally we only offer a 7-day free trial, but with my code you get 14 days free, no card required, cancel anytime." This is your single strongest line. Say it early, say it twice.
The no-brainer line"It's free leads, free customers and free profits. Sign up now, it's a no-brainer." Use this after you've given the 14-day pitch to make the decision feel obvious.
The proof line"We have proof of every lead. Each one comes with the full contact details, so you know it's a real lead, not a guess." This removes the "are these real?" objection before it's asked.
The exclusivity line"You get your own daily allocation. Nobody else gets your leads, and you're never sent the same lead twice." Frame it as a private allocation, not a shared feed.
The Print & Post line"We even offer auto Print & Post. We send your leaflet or letter out for you, and you track it live. You just open your leads and make the calls." This adds a service layer competitors don't have.
Objection round-upPrice → "14 days free, then from £25 a week. One extra customer covers it." Busy → "Five minutes each morning. Open leads at 9am, call the strongest two." Referrals → "This is extra, on top, and it's free. Nothing to lose."

7. Match every lead to the right script

Removal companyUse the Moving script in Tools: fresh under-offer homes, first call usually wins the move.
Solicitor / estate adminUse the Probate script: grants from the official register, first contact wins the file.
Accountant / business servicesUse the New Business script: fresh registrations, first contact wins the onboarding.
Builder / tradesUse the Planning script: approvals ready to spend, first quote gets the site visit.
Cleaning / IT / facilitiesUse the Tenders script: matched public contracts, far less competition.
Not sure?Use the General script and match them on the call. Every lead type uses the same 14-day code pitch, so you never need to memorise six different opens.

How the wheel works

  • Every 50 retained signups = one spin.
  • Retained = customers who paid their 2nd invoice and stayed (same as your £25 commission).
  • Prizes are paid straight to your balance the moment you win.
  • Tiers escalate: 50 → up to £200, 100 → up to £500, 200+ → up to £1000.
  • Fair odds: each prize has an equal chance.
  • If a counted customer refunds, their count is removed and any unclaimed spin is reset.
