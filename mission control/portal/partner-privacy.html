3. Data about members you invite or refer

When you use the invite tool or your referral link, we process the contact details you provide (such as name, email, company and any area/segment) to send the invitation and to attribute and administer the referral. You must have a lawful basis to provide those details to us, and must not send us data you are not permitted to share.

4. Lawful bases

  • Contract - to operate the Programme and pay Commission.
  • Legal obligation - e.g. tax, accounting and anti-fraud checks.
  • Legitimate interests - to run and improve the Programme, prevent fraud and secure our systems.
  • Consent - where required, e.g. certain marketing; you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Sharing

  • Service providers who help us run the Programme, including payment processing (Stripe), email delivery (Brevo), cloud hosting, and banks used to make payouts.
  • Professional advisers (accountants, lawyers) and authorities where required by law.
  • Some providers may process data outside the UK; where so, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

We keep account and referral data while you participate and for as long afterwards as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements (generally up to 6 years). KYC/ID data is kept only as long as necessary to verify identity and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these, email hello@9amleads.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls and staff confidentiality. No method is 100% secure; please keep your credentials safe.

9. Cookies

Our portal uses essential cookies/local storage for login, attribution and security. See our main Privacy Policy for details.

10. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. Material changes will be notified in the dashboard or by email.
