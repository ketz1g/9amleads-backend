The 9amLeads Origin

Our founder spent years running a service-based business, burning cash on lead generation platforms that promised the world but delivered recycled leads — the same enquiries being sold to five other businesses. Every morning felt like a lottery. Would the phone ring? Would the leads be real? Would we be first?

Born from frustration with expensive lead platforms, 9amLeads was built on a different philosophy: exclusive, fresh, and predictable. No bidding. No sharing. No wasted time. Just leads that arrive at 9am, every single day, before anyone else knows they exist.

What started as a solution for one business became a platform serving 500+ UK companies across Moving, Probate, New Business, Planning, and Tenders.
