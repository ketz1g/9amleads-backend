Appendix · Risk & diligence

Risk, honestly handled before you ask.

⚠️ Source dependency

We rely on sources we don't own. Mitigation: multiple sources per vertical, resilient fallbacks, and a roadmap toward first-party/opt-in data.

⚠️ Trial → paid conversion

No paid customers yet. Mitigation: product is self-serve and verifiable; the round funds structured conversion (demos, follow-up, affiliate closes); benchmarks are conservative.

⚠️ Competition / copycats

Freshness + door-number quality + integrated print are hard to copy cheaply. Mitigation: depth-first domination, brand + trade-body partnerships, fast iteration.

⚠️ Churn assumptions

Model assumes ~8%→5%. Mitigation: weekly billing surfaces issues early; daily value creates habit; upsell path keeps accounts growing.

⚠️ Single founder

Built the entire platform solo. Mitigation: automating aggressively + advisory support and a first-hire plan funded by the round's sales budget.

⚠️ Small round / valuation

£25k for 10% = £250k post. Mitigation: fair for pre-revenue-with-product; SEIS relief halves investor cost; low burn means the round buys real milestones.
