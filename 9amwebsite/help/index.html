Getting started

Set Up in 5 Minutes

1

Create your free account

Sign up at portal/#signup — no card required. Choose your email, password and company name.

2

Pick your lead type

Choose Moving, Probate, New Business, Planning or Tenders — or combine several. Each has its own daily allowance and delivery.

3

Set your target areas

Enter the postcode areas (moving, planning) or counties/industries (probate, new business) or keywords (tenders) you want to work.

4

Get your first delivery at 9am

Your fresh opportunities land in your inbox and dashboard at 9am the next working day — scored, with draft outreach and source links.

5

Follow up and convert

Use the suggested outreach, marketing templates and optional Print & Post. Track contacts, quotes and wins in your dashboard.
