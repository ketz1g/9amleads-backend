The Conversion Playbook

Turn Every Lead Into A Job You Win

1

Contact Within 48 Hours

Executors decide fast. A grant is public, but the family decides who to appoint in the first days. Our lead includes the executor details and Drafted outreach. Move within 48 hours.

2

Offer A Fixed-Fee First Step

Grieving families fear the meter running. A clear fixed-fee first meeting or grant handling removes that fear and makes the decision easy.

3

Ask About The Whole Estate

Do not just take the grant. Ask about the house, the will, the accounts, the clearance. Each "yes" is a new instruction worth more than the first.

4

Stay In Touch For 12 Months

Estate administration takes months. A nurture sequence (check-ins, tax deadlines) keeps you the obvious choice for every step as it comes.

Estate Agents
