About 9amLeads

Built to Give UK Businesses a Predictable Pipeline of Fresh Opportunities

9amLeads delivers fresh UK business opportunities every morning at 9am — moving, probate, new business, planning and public-sector tender leads in the postcode areas you choose. Here is who we are, what we believe, and what we want to achieve.

Our Mission

Why 9amLeads Exists

We started 9amLeads because we saw the same problem everywhere: great businesses spending their most productive hours searching for opportunities instead of closing deals — or paying expensive lead marketplaces where several companies fight over the same enquiry.

Fresh Data, Every Morning

Opportunities are prioritised from the last 24 hours of live public data and delivered to your inbox at 9am — so your team starts the day ready to act, not searching.

Postcode Targeting

You choose the UK postcode areas you work in. No wasted, out-of-area leads. Change or expand your areas anytime.

Transparency, Not a Marketplace

9amLeads is a subscription data feed, not a lead marketplace. Every opportunity carries source information so you can verify it yourself, and you're never sent the same one twice.

Our Goals

What We Want to Achieve

We measure our success by our customers' success. These are the goals that drive every decision we make.

Give Every Business a Fair Chance

Most lead generation sites sell the same enquiry to 3–5 businesses and turn the customer into a bidding war. We want to build something fairer — a platform where every business gets its own daily allocation of fresh opportunities, with the tools and templates to convert them.

Make Prospecting Predictable

No more wondering where the next customer is coming from. A fixed weekly price, a consistent 9am delivery, and a simple way to track contacts, quotes and wins — so building your pipeline becomes a routine, not a scramble.

Be Honest About What We Deliver

We won't promise territory exclusivity, guaranteed ROI or "unlimited" leads. We promise fresh, sourced, deliverable opportunities at 9am — and we work hard to keep improving freshness, quality and coverage.

Keep Improving With Real Feedback

The founder personally reads every piece of customer feedback. Every feature, template and improvement is driven by real conversations with the businesses that use 9amLeads.

What We Believe In

The Values Behind the Product

Freshness first

Opportunities are prioritised from the last 24 hours. When supply is lower, selected lead types may use the latest suitable opportunities available.

Source transparency

Where available, every opportunity includes its original source link so you can review and verify it yourself.

No shared-lead marketplace model

We don't auction enquiries to the highest bidder. You get your own daily allocation, and we never send you the same opportunity twice.

Postcode targeting

You choose the areas you serve, so your team only sees opportunities worth acting on.

Tools to convert, not just leads

Opportunity scoring, marketing templates, suggested outreach, built-in lead management and optional Print & Post.

Every lead type on this platform — Moving, Probate, New Business, Planning and Tenders — comes from real experience understanding what these businesses actually need to grow.
