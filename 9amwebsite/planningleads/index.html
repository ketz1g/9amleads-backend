The Conversion Playbook

Turn Every Lead Into A Job You Win

1

Quote Within Days Of Approval

The first builder to quote usually gets the site visit. Our lead includes the project spec and Drafted outreach. Move within days, before they ring anyone else.

2

Offer A Free Site Survey

3

Itemise The Full Job

Extensions need groundworks, steelwork, electrics and roofing. Itemise the full scope so the price looks complete and the client stops phoning for comparison quotes.

4

Put A Date On The Quote

"This price stands for 30 days" creates urgency. Approved homeowners decide fast. A dated quote matches their pace and wins.

The Psychology Of The Yes

Why Buyers Say Yes When You Call First

Commitment Bias

They have already paid for drawings and fees. They are committed to building. You are not persuading them to start, just to choose you. Easiest sell in construction.

Show Similar Projects

Photos of a near-identical extension you built in their area is the single most persuasive thing you can send. Proof beats promises.

Anchoring Works

Quote the full scope first, then break it down. Presenting the complete job first sets the anchor. The client compares against your total, not competitors.

Diary Scarcity

"We are booking six weeks ahead" is a real, gentle push. Approved homeowners fear losing their summer build slot more than they fear paying.

Four Trades That Profit from Planning Leads

Every approved planning application creates work for specific trades. Here is why each one needs to be first to contact the applicant.

Builders

Every approved extension or new build needs a main contractor. Planning applications tell you exactly who is building what and where before they have chosen their builder. Be the first to quote and you win more work.

Architects

Homeowners need architectural drawings and design statements before they can submit planning applications. Spot applications that need professional design input and offer your services early in the process.
