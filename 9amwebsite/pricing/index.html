Territory Exclusivity

Are the leads exclusive?

We do not want multiple identical businesses all chasing the same local lead. That is why 9amLeads is designed around territories and business categories.

For local lead types, we aim to offer one business per industry per territory.

If a removal company buys EN1 moving leads, we do not sell the same EN1 moving lead package to another removal company.

However, the same area may be available to a different type of business.

A planning lead could be useful to one builder, one architect, and one kitchen company, but not ten builders all competing for the same job.

This helps keep the service fair, valuable, and more useful for customers.
