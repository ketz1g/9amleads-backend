An honest note on tender leads
Tenders are public opportunities - by law they must be, and any supplier can see them on the official portals. We never re-sell your matched feed; what we give you is the ones relevant to your business, delivered early each morning so you can respond first, with bid summaries, full documentation and deadline tracking to help you win.

One honest point on volume: some areas and sectors genuinely publish fewer tenders than others, so your daily count varies by location - that's the real UK market, and we'd rather be upfront than oversell.
