# WORKFLOWS.md

## Workflow: Lead Generation

Steps:
1. Identify target audience
2. Create outreach plan
3. Generate messaging
4. Present for approval
5. Execute after approval
6. Track results
7. Optimise

Automation potential: High

## Workflow: Digital Product Funnel

Steps:
1. Generate traffic
2. Offer entry product
3. Upsell to higher value product
4. Follow up
5. Track conversions

Automation potential: Very high
