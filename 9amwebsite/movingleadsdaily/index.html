Why These Leads Win You Work

Why These Leads Grow Your Business

A newly listed property means a household that is planning to move and starting to choose who will handle it. Here is why these are the strongest leads a removal company can buy.

You Call First. You Win First.

You receive the address the moment a property is listed. Often within hours. By 9am you are calling before competitors even know the family is moving. In removals, the first quote usually becomes the booking.

Ready To Buy, Not Just Browsing

These homeowners have exchanged contracts. They have a fixed move date and a real deadline. They will book someone this week, not next month. You are selling to someone who is already buying.

Door-Numbered & In Your Territory

Every lead is a real address in your chosen postcodes. No wasted calls 50 miles away. Your van can actually reach these homes. And you know it before you dial.

A Job That Compounds

One move leads to packing, storage, furniture assembly and future referrals. Every booking you land from a fresh lead is the start of a customer, not just a job.

The Conversion Playbook

Turn Every Lead Into A Job You Win

1

Call Within 24 Hours

A moving lead loses value fast. The homeowners are comparing quotes for a matter of days. Use the Drafted outreach message, then follow up by phone before noon. Speed is your unfair advantage.

2

Quote The Whole Move

Do not quote a price. Quote a plan. Fixed date, fixed price, named crew, everything included. Removal companies that itemise and guarantee win more jobs at better margins.

3

Print & Post To Their Door

One call gets voicemail. A letter on the doormat gets read. Send a branded flyer the same day. While the quote decision is still fresh in their mind.

4

Follow Up Three Times

One touch rarely closes. Call, WhatsApp and mail. 70% of moving jobs go to whoever follows up the most professionally. Be that company.

The Psychology Of The Yes

Why Buyers Say Yes When You Call First

Urgency Beats Indecision

A fixed move date forces a decision. Homeowners under deadline pressure choose the first credible option that removes risk. That is you, if you are in front of them.

Certainty Beats Cheapest

People do not pick the cheapest removal. They pick the one that feels safe. Guarantee, insurance, a named crew and clear steps remove the fear of "will they turn up?".

Social Proof Wins Trust

Mention neighbours you have moved in their postcode. "We moved three families on your road this year" is the strongest closing line in removals.

Give First, Get Back

Offer a free packing checklist or tip sheet. Reciprocity is real. Homeowners who receive value from you before booking are far more likely to return the favour.
