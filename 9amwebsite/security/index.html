Security

Security You Can Depend On

We take the security of your account, your data and your leads seriously. Here's how we protect what matters.

How we protect you

Security by Design

Encryption in Transit

All traffic between your browser, our site and the 9amLeads API is encrypted with TLS/SSL. Every connection is HTTPS.

Secure Storage

Data is stored in managed, access-controlled storage. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.

Access Controls

Access to production systems is limited to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis, with audit logging.

Reliable Infrastructure

We run on managed cloud infrastructure (Render) with continuous deployment, monitoring and automatic restart on failure.

Backups

Production data is backed up regularly so your account and lead history can be restored quickly if ever needed.

Compliance-First

We process data in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. See our GDPR page for details.

Our Security Commitments

Continuous monitoring. We monitor platform health around the clock. If a service degrades, our systems alert us immediately so we can respond fast.

Least-privilege access. Only the minimum number of people, with the minimum permissions needed, can access production data and infrastructure.

Honest data handling. Lead data is sourced from public registers. We never sell your data, and we never sell your leads to third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.

Secure development. Changes are reviewed and deployed through automated pipelines, reducing the risk of accidental exposure.

Prompt response. If you ever spot a security concern or a vulnerability, email us immediately at hello@9amleads.com and we'll take it seriously.
