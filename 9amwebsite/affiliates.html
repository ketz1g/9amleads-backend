FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to join?

Yes. Completely free, and it takes under two minutes. Name, email, a password and a short voice test. No fees, no minimums, no commitment. You get your code and share link instantly.

What is the Wheel of Fortune?

Every 50 retained sign-ups you earn a spin. Prizes start at £50-£200, then £100-£500, then £200-£1,000, with fair odds on every wheel. The moment you win, the prize goes straight into your payout balance. It's a bonus on top of your £25 per sign-up.

How do I get paid?

Your £25 commission is created once a customer you referred pays their second invoice. It clears after the qualifying period and is paid out on the monthly cycle. Your total. And exactly which customer it came from. Is always visible in your dashboard.

How much do I earn per customer?

£25 for every customer you refer who pays their second invoice, plus your wheel spins every 50 sign-ups. No cap and no time limit on how many customers you can refer. Every qualifying sign-up is worth a clean £25.

Do my referrals really get 14 days free?

Yes. Any customer who uses your code at signup gets a 14-day free trial instead of the standard 7 days. That's the single easiest way to turn a conversation into a sign-up: you're handing them a better deal than they can get anywhere else.

Can I share my code anywhere?

Absolutely. Use your personal short link anywhere. In person, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, email, or on your own website. It remembers visitors for 30 days, so a sign-up that happens later still counts to you.

Do I need to know anything about leads?

No. Your dashboard gives you ready-made scripts, emails, texts, social posts and follow-up plans for every lead type. You just copy and share.

I run a trade association or business network

We run dedicated partnerships with trade bodies and business networks. Members get a boosted rate on every sign-up they bring. Email hello@9amleads.com and we'll set up your association's code and shareable materials.

When do I start earning?

Around a month after they sign up, when they pay their second invoice (14 days free, then two weekly payments). It is a sign they're staying with 9amLeads. Your commission is tracked live in your dashboard from the moment it's earned.

How do I contact support?

Email hello@9amleads.com or use the contact form below. We normally reply within a few hours, and always within one working day.
