Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 May 2026

1. Introduction

9am Leads Ltd ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.9amleads.com and use our lead generation services.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide: When you sign up for our service, we collect your name, company name, email address, phone number, and target areas of interest.

Information collected automatically: We may collect certain information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and usage patterns on our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our lead generation services
  • Deliver daily lead sheets to your email at 9am
  • Communicate with you about your account, subscription, and service updates
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent, where required by law)
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sources for Leads

The leads we provide are sourced from publicly available data sources including:

  • Rightmove and Zoopla (property listings marked SSTC)
  • Companies House (new company registrations)
  • Gov.uk Probate Register
  • UK local authority planning portals
  • Gov.uk Contracts Finder

We do not collect or store personal data beyond what is publicly available from these sources.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist us in operating our platform (e.g., email delivery services, payment processors)
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities where required by law
  • Professional advisers including lawyers and accountants

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

8. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Request portability of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@9amleads.com.

9. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites.

11. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the UK. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with UK data protection law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or through a prominent notice on our website.
