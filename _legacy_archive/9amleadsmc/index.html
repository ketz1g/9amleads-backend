Lead Scrapers

Configure and run scrapers for each business type
How scrapers work:
Each business type has a pre-configured Apify actor that finds the right kind of leads.
You can customise the search with your own instructions and set how many results you want.
After scraping, the system enriches results with owner/director, marketing, and sales emails where possible.

Business Types:
Moving Leads → Removal companies, man-and-van operators, couriers
Probate Leads → Solicitors, will-writers, estate administrators
New Business → Accountants, web designers, B2B services
Planning Permission → Builders, architects, kitchen fitters, tradespeople
Public Tenders → Tender-ready businesses (IT, construction, cleaning)
