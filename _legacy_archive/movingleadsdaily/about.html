About Moving Leads Daily

We help UK removal companies find homeowners before their competitors do — with real-time property data delivered daily.

Our Story

Moving Leads Daily was founded in 2026 after we saw removal companies spending thousands on Google Ads and quote sites while their competitors were quietly booking jobs by being first to contact homeowners.

We built a system that scans the UK's leading property portals every morning and delivers fresh addresses of people who are definitely moving — so removal companies can contact them before anyone else.

Today we serve 50+ removal companies across the UK, from solo operators with one van to multi-vehicle operations covering entire cities.

🎯

Accuracy First

We use verified property data from Rightmove and Zoopla — not estimated or inferred data.

⚡

Speed Matters

Leads are delivered within 24 hours of a property being listed. Being first is everything.

🤝

Exclusive Territories

Only one removal company per area. No competing against others for the same leads.

Our Mission

We believe removal companies should win jobs based on the quality of their service — not who spends the most on advertising.

By providing exclusive, real-time moving leads at a fraction of the cost of traditional advertising, we level the playing field for independent removal companies across the UK.
