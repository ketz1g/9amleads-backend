# 9amLeads — Pitch Deck Outline (10 slides)

> Tip: 8-10 slides, ~3 minutes talking. Use real screenshots of the dashboard + a delivered lead + a real Stannp order.

## Slide 1 — Title
- **9amLeads** — "Fresh local opportunities, delivered every morning at 9am"
- One-line: "We give local trades & professional firms first contact on new work."
- Founder name + contact

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Local firms waste hours cold-calling and buy **stale, outdated** databases.
- The winner is whoever contacts the prospect **first** — but there's no reliable source of *fresh* opportunities.
- Each opportunity is high value: removal job £1,500-£2,000 · probate instruction £5k-£15k.

## Slide 3 — Solution
- 9amLeads **scrapes + verifies 5 lead sources daily**, enriches every lead (door number, street, town/county, postcode), and **delivers at 9am sharp**.
- Dashboard screenshot: fresh leads, area filters, daily limits, "Select" to mail.

## Slide 4 — The 5 verticals (each a real market)
- Moving · Probate · Planning & Construction · New Business · Tenders
- Show one example lead per vertical with the full address (proves quality).

## Slide 5 — How we make money (two streams)
- **Subscription (weekly)**: Starter £25/wk = **£100/mo** · Pro £196/mo · Enterprise £396/mo
- **Print & Post**: £1.99 flyer / £1.49 letter / £2.99 pack — **we roughly double revenue per customer** (~£100/mo sub → ~£200/mo with P&P)
- Real screenshots: the Print & Post preview + a sent Stannp order.

## Slide 6 — Traction (real, live)
- Product **fully built & shipping** — daily 9am delivery, Print & Post (real mail via Stannp), affiliate programme
- 76 accounts · 8 real trial firms · daily delivery running
- Screenshots: live dashboard, real sent order, delivery log.

## Slide 7 — Market
- UK removals, probate, planning/construction, ~70k new businesses/mo, public tenders
- TAM/SAM/SOM: three lines — the niche is deep and underserved.

## Slide 8 — The model (your numbers)
- **100 customers on Starter = £10,000/month**; **~£20,000/month with Print & Post**
- **1,000 customers = £100,000/month → up to £200,000/month with Print & Post**
- Unit economics: ARPU ~£110/mo blended, CAC ~£150, **payback in ~1-2 months**.

## Slide 9 — The Ask
- **£25,000 for 10%** (post-money £250k)
- Use of funds: marketing £12k · sales £8k · sampling/tools £3k · contingency £2k
- What it buys: ~150-170 paying customers → **£15k-£17k/mo subscription, ~£30k+/mo with Print & Post**.

## Slide 10 — Why now / close
- Product done & shipping; the missing piece is **marketing & sales**.
- 50 warm trial accounts already in the funnel.
- "Help us take 9amLeads to 200 paying customers and £100k+ run-rate."

---

## Talking points
- Lead with the **live product** — don't show slides, show the dashboard and a real printed letter.
- Emphasise **"first contact"** — that's the core value and the defensible moat (freshness).
- On valuation: £250k post-money for a working, shipping, 5-vertical B2B SaaS is a fair angel entry.
