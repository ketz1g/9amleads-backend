# 9amLeads — UK Investor Shortlist (£25k for 10%, SEIS-friendly)

## (a) Angel networks & syndicates — best fit for a £25k SEIS seed

**1. Angels Den** [V] — *Angel network + platform, UK-wide*
- Average angel ticket **£25k** — your exact size. 27,000+ investors, 1,000+ startups funded.
- Path: open founder application → `https://app.angelsden.com/apply` · site `https://www.angelsden.com/founders`
- Why: deliberately built for small SEIS/EIS deals; few platforms serve this size well.
- Caveat: platform matchmaking — quality varies; do your own diligence on anyone who engages.

**2. Angel Investment Network (AIN)** [V] — *Angel marketplace (UKBAA member), UK-wide*
- Investor tickets from £1k up; no minimum raise.
- Path: free entrepreneur signup → `https://www.angelinvestmentnetwork.co.uk/entrepreneur/register`
- Why: cheap, wide reach to SEIS-hunting angels; good way to test the offer.
- Caveat: pay-to-promote tiers; low signal-to-noise; vet every enquiry.

**3. Cambridge Angels** [V] — *Angel syndicate, Cambridge + national*
- No pitch/carry fees to companies; free one-on-one "office hours" feedback even before applying.
- Path: `https://www.cambridgeangels.com/apply-to-cambridge-angels`
- Why: operator angels who understand SaaS subscriptions; 150+ companies backed.
- Caveat: members are invitation-only — competitive; use office hours first.

**4. Envestors / Envestry** [V] — *Private investor network, SEIS/EIS-native, UK-wide*
- Per-investor from ~£10k; comfortable with micro-SEIS raises; £180m+ raised since 2004.
- Path: `https://envestors.envestry.com/raising` (FCA-regulated)
- Caveat: recent emphasis on Innovator Founder visa work — confirm current UK-founder deal flow.

**5. London Business Angels (LBA)** [#] — *Angel network, London + national*
- Angel tickets ~£10–20k; network often funds larger rounds — a £25k raise is small for them (still worth pitching if they like the model).
- Path: founders application form on `https://www.londonbusinessangels.com` (re-verify it's open).
- Caveat: could not verify current intake this session.

**6. Angel Investors Bristol (AIB)** [V] — *UKBAA-affiliated, SEIS/EIS specialist, South West & South Wales*
- Rounds ~£50–250k via SPV; monthly pitch slots.
- Path: self-assessment first at `https://angel.scoreapp.com`; if "investment ready" you get the apply link.
- Why: explicitly SEIS/EIS + SaaS + traction-friendly; loves grant/co-investment matches.
- Caveat: **strict geography** — needs a real South West (incl. Glos/Wilts) presence; 5% introducer fee.

**7. NorthInvest** [V] — *Angel network, Leeds / North of England (Angel Group of the Year 2022)*
- ~220 angels; early-stage tech incl. micro rounds; free investment-readiness coaching first.
- Path: open application → `https://share.hsforms.com/1t91-QEf8RDKYElEdgn-uEw3e2w7` · `https://northinvest.co.uk/raise/`
- Why: the most accessible North-network path for a first formal round.
- Caveat: generally wants the business/team based in the North.

**8. GC Angels** [#] — *Angel network, Greater Manchester / North West*
- Early-stage; grants + equity blended.
- Path: online form at `https://www.gcangels.co.uk` (re-verify).
- Caveat: regional focus.

**9. Minerva Business Angels** [#] — *Angel network, West Midlands (Warwick) + national*
- Rounds typically £50k–£1m; SEIS/EIS deals; long-established.
- Path: `https://www.minerva-ba.co.uk` (re-verify current intake).
- Caveat: minimums may exceed £25k — ask.

**10. UKBAA (UK Business Angels Association)** [V] — *Trade body / umbrella (not an investor)*
- Runs Angel Hubs + TechInvest pitch showcases (with British Business Bank).
- Path: regional hub list → `https://ukbaa.org.uk/membership/angel-hubs/` · `enquiries@ukbaa.org.uk`
- Why: gateway to member networks and SEIS-aware angels; unlocks warm intros.

## (b) Equity crowdfunding — only for a bigger round, not £25k

**11. Crowd for Angels** [V] — some real sub-£100k SEIS raises; investors from £25. Path: `https://www.crowdforangels.com/funding`. **Caveat:** fees make a £25k campaign poor value — use at £75k+.

**12. Crowdcube** [#] — campaigns now cluster £250k–£5m; fee structure uneconomical at £25k. **Keep for the next round.**

**13. Seedrs (Republic Europe)** [#] — historically the small-SEIS/EIS platform but now skews larger. **Keep for the £150k+ round.**

## (c) Accelerators & free founder support (non-dilutive + investor access)

**14. Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** [V] — free; credits + mentorship. `https://foundershub.startups.microsoft.com`
**15. Barclays Eagle Labs** [V] — free; regional labs + demo days + warm angel intros. `https://labs.uk.barclays`
**16. Google for Startups UK** — free programmes/mentorship. `https://startups.google.com`
**17. SETsquared** — university incubator partnership (Bath/Bristol/Exeter/Southampton/Surrey) with an angel-network arm. `https://www.setsquared.co.uk`

## (d) Government-backed — non-dilutive cash & scheme support

**18. Start Up Loans (British Business Bank)** [V] — **up to £25,000** unsecured personal loan at ~7.5% fixed, 1–5 yr, + 12 months free mentoring. Eligible while trading <60 months. Apply: `https://apply.startuploans.co.uk/s/register` · `https://www.startuploans.co.uk`. **Note:** it's a personal loan — disclose to equity investors.
**19. HMRC SEIS Advance Assurance** [V] — free; the unlock for every angel above. Apply: `https://www.gov.uk/guidance/venture-capital-schemes-apply-for-advance-assurance`
**20. Innovate UK grant competitions** — feasibility grants ~£25k+; must show real innovation (lead-gen SaaS is a stretch — try later with an AI/data angle). `https://www.apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk`
**21. British Business Bank Finance Hub** — free eligibility checks + lender directory. `https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/finance-hub`
**22. Growth Hub / Business Support Helpline (England)** — free advice + regional micro-grants (£1k–£10k). Phone 0300 456 3565 · `https://www.gov.uk/business-support-helpline`

## (e) Sector & warm-operator communities (customers AND small angel cheques)

**23. BAR — British Association of Removers** [V] — your #1 buyer segment. Partner/affiliate route + annual conference & regional events. `https://bar.co.uk/partnerships-and-affiliates/` · `https://bar.co.uk/contact-us/`. *"BAR-accredited fresh leads" is your strongest investor narrative.*
**24. FMB — Federation of Master Builders** [V] — associate membership £642/yr+VAT gets you into regional meetings of builders (buyers + operator angels). `membership@fmb.org.uk` · `https://www.fmb.org.uk`
**25. Legal Geek** [V] — legal-tech community/conference (probate-firm buyers + legal angels). `https://www.legalgeek.co` (now part of Centellic).
**26. STEP — Society of Trust & Estate Practitioners** [#] — probate practitioners' body; attend/exhibit regionally. `https://www.step.org`
**27. Local operator angels via your own customer base** — the 8 paying-trial firms + BAR/FMB contacts are the warmest £2.5–5k cheques you'll ever get.

---

## Recommended sequencing
1. **File SEIS Advance Assurance now** (free, ~weeks) — every investor below asks for it.
2. **Start Up Loans application in parallel** — up to £25k non-dilutive (combines with the equity round).
3. Pitch **regional networks you genuinely have presence in** (AIB, NorthInvest, GC, Minerva, LBA).
4. Use **Angels Den / AIN / Envestors** for national frictionless SEIS reach.
5. Treat **Crowdcube/Seedrs** as your *next* round (£150k+), not this one.
6. Mine **BAR / FMB / Legal Geek + existing customers** for warm operator money alongside.
