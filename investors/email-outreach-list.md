## Bulk-send playbook (important — do this right)
1. **Batch it:** send 5–10 per day max, not one giant blast — blind blasting gets flagged as spam and burns the domain's deliverability (and reputations travel in angel circles).
2. **Personalise one line per recipient** (their network/region/reason) — a pure mail-merge with no personalisation converts near zero.
3. **BCC** any genuine bulk copy, include your real name + company + a working reply-to, and an opt-out line.
4. **Content:** subject = value + SEIS; body = 5 lines (one-pager link, live-demo link, £25k for 10%, SEIS badge) — see `outreach-emails.md` Variant A.
5. **Track every send** in `investor-tracker.csv` (add rows, mark Contacted/Reply).
6. **Never** buy lists or email individuals whose address you can't confirm; use only published business contacts (this list) — stays inside UK B2B cold-email norms.
7. Expect most real SEIS money to come from **forms + intros + warm referrals**, not cold mail — treat bulk email as a top-of-funnel numbers game while the warm routes (BAR/FMB, existing customers, local networks) do the heavy lifting.

## Realistic expectation
Verified public **emails** ≈ 20; another ~15 free intake **forms** = pitchable today. Total ~35 routes, no platform fees. Individual SEIS angels rarely publish emails, so don't chase "individual angel email lists" offered online — they're usually stale or scraped.
