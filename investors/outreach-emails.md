- **Day 5–7 (Variant A/C):** "Just following up — happy to send the one-pager + 12-month model if that's easier than a call. We're closing the round over the next few weeks."
- **Day 14 (final):** "Last nudge — if the timing's not right no problem. If you'd like to stay in the loop for our next round (post-traction, higher valuation), say the word."

## Inbound checklist (what to send the moment someone says "yes")
1. One-pager PDF · 2. 12-month model · 3. Cap-table one-liner ("£25k = 10%, post-money £250k, founder keeps 90%") · 4. Live demo calendar link · 5. (When they're serious) Data room: see `data-room-checklist.md`.

## Do / don't
- **Do** open with the live product; lead the demo not the deck.
- **Do** name a real delivered lead/letter in every email.
- **Don't** over-share financials before an NDA/interest — the one-pager numbers are enough.
- **Don't** pitch Crowdcube/Seedrs at £25k (fees); keep them for the £150k+ round.
