## Problem
- Local trades & professional firms (removal companies, probate solicitors, builders, roofers, accountants) waste hours cold-calling and pay for stale, outdated databases.
- The winner is whoever **contacts the prospect first**. Today there's no reliable way to get fresh, accurate opportunities the same morning they appear.
- Each of our verticals is high-value: one removal job ≈ £1,500–£2,000 · one probate instruction ≈ £5,000–£15,000 in fees.

## Solution
9amLeads **scrapes + verifies 5 lead sources daily**, enriches each lead with door number, street, town/county and full postcode, and **delivers them at 9am sharp** to each customer's dashboard — in their chosen areas, up to their daily limit.

- **Moving** — newly listed residential & commercial properties (door-numbered for removals)
- **Probate** — fresh probate opportunities for solicitors, valuers, house-clearance firms
- **Planning** — new planning applications & projects for builders/trades
- **New Business** — newly registered companies needing services
- **Tenders** — public & private tender notices
