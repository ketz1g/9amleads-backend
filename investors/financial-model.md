# 9amLeads — 12-Month Financial Projection
£25,000 raise. Pricing: Starter £25/wk = **£100/mo** · Pro £196/mo · Enterprise £396/mo. **Four revenue streams** (below) — Print & Post and Auto-Send are **built & live**; Bulk Postage is **built & live**, with volume ramping as the archive pool fills.

**Growth curve (owner's target): 100 paying by M3 · 300 by M6 · 500 by M9 · 600 by M10 · 1,000 by M12.**

## The four revenue streams
1. **Subscriptions** — weekly cash-flow: Starter £100/mo · Pro £196/mo · Enterprise £396/mo, with natural Starter → Pro → Enterprise upgrades.
2. **Print & Post** (per lead, pay-as-you-go) — A5 leaflet £2.99 · A4 letter £2.49 · leaflet + letter £4.49.
3. **Auto-Send** — automatically prints & posts every new lead after the 9am delivery (charged per piece). Hands-free recurring print revenue.
4. **Bulk Postage** — exclusive never-sent archive lead packs, printed & posted: 100 = £249 · 250 = £622.50 · 500 = £1,245 · 1,000 = £2,490 (≈£2.49/lead). High-value one-off Pro-upsell orders. Pool volume ramps as the archive fills.

## Assumptions
- **Blended subscription ARPU ≈ £150/mo** — mix of Starter £100/mo · Pro £196/mo · Enterprise £396/mo (e.g. ~60% Starter / 30% Pro / 10% Enterprise = £158/mo; we use £150 to stay conservative). Higher-value customers (Pro/Enterprise) materially lift revenue above the Starter baseline.
- **Print & Auto-Send attach** ≈ **+£135/mo per customer** at target attach (~0.9× the subscription figure) as customers auto-mail or follow up leads.
- **Bulk packs** ≈ **+£15/mo per customer** across the base at scale (occasional one-off orders; ramps as the pool fills).
- **All-streams blended ≈ £300/mo per customer** at target attach (≈£150 sub + £135 print/auto-send + £15 bulk).
- **Acquisition**: paid channels ~£150 CAC in months 1-3; then the **affiliate/staff engine (~£0 CAC)** carries most growth. Revenue reinvested after month 3.
- **Churn**: ~8%/mo early, improving to ~5%/mo.

## Why the plan mix matters
- A customer on **Pro (£196/mo)** earns ~2× a Starter; **Enterprise (£396/mo)** earns ~4×.
- As customers need more leads/areas they **upgrade** — subscription ARPU naturally rises over time (expansion revenue).
- Print/Auto-Send/Bulk make each customer worth roughly **2× their subscription** at target attach — every delivered lead can become a posted letter or a future bulk order.
- Even a conservative mix keeps blended subscription ARPU at £150/mo; pushing more Pro/Enterprise lifts it toward £200+/mo, with print streams on top.

## Cash & profitability
- £25,000 raised; covers marketing/sales for roughly months 1-3 (~100 customers).
- From month 3 the business is **strongly cash-positive** (~£30k+/mo all-streams revenue) — growth thereafter funded by revenue + the ~£0-CAC affiliate engine.
- **Break-even: month 3.** Payback per customer: ~£150 CAC vs ~£150-£300/mo customer value → **under 1 month**.

## The affiliate/staff engine (key to the curve)
- Staff, agents & customers refer businesses they know → earn **£25/sign-up** + reward wheel up to £1,000.
- Referred customers get a 14-day free trial — warm, pre-qualified.
- This is the **low-CAC scaling lever** that makes 600-1,000 customers in year 1 realistic.

# 3-Year Outlook

> For a £25k seed raise, the **12-month model above is the core**. This 3-year view shows the ambition and the path to a large, profitable company. It is **aspirational** — built on the affiliate engine scaling + revenue reinvestment, and it's what a follow-on (Series A) round would be based on.

## Assumptions
- Year 1 ends at **1,000 paying customers** (the 12-month model).
- Year 2-3 growth driven by: the affiliate/staff engine (~£0 CAC), expanding marketing budget from revenue, upsells (Starter → Pro → Enterprise), and 2-3 new verticals.
- All-streams value per customer rises toward **£340-360/mo** by Y3 as the mix shifts to Pro/Enterprise and print/bulk attach deepens.
- Bulk pack pool volume scales up through Y1-Y2 as the archive grows.
- Gross margin ~60-70% (subscription is high-margin; Print & Post ~40-50%).
