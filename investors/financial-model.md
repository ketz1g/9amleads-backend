# 9amLeads — 12-Month Financial Projection
£25,000 raise. Pricing: Starter £25/wk = **£100/mo** · Pro £196/mo · Enterprise £396/mo. Print & Post **doubles** revenue per customer.
**Growth curve (owner's target): 100 paying by M3 · 300 by M6 · 500 by M9 · 600 by M10 · 1,000 by M12.**

## Assumptions
- **Blended ARPU ≈ £150/mo** — a mix of plans: Starter £100/mo · Pro £196/mo · Enterprise £396/mo (e.g. ~60% Starter / 30% Pro / 10% Enterprise = £158/mo; we use £150 to stay conservative). Higher-value customers (Pro/Enterprise) materially lift revenue above the £100 Starter baseline.
- **Print & Post**: customers who mail add on top → total ~**2× the subscription figure** at good attach.
- **Acquisition**: paid channels ~£150 CAC in months 1-3; then the **affiliate/staff engine (~£0 CAC)** carries most growth. Revenue reinvested after month 3.
- **Churn**: ~8%/mo early, improving to ~5%/mo.

## Milestones investors will ask about (blended ARPU)
- **M3 — 100 paying** → £15k/mo subs, ~£30k/mo with Print & Post
- **M6 — 300 paying** → £45k/mo, ~£90k/mo
- **M9 — 500 paying** → £75k/mo, ~£150k/mo
- **M10 — 600 paying** → £90k/mo, ~£180k/mo
- **M12 — 1,000 paying** → £150k/mo, ~£300k/mo (~£1.8M-£3.6M/yr run-rate)

## Why the plan mix matters
- A customer on **Pro (£196/mo)** earns ~2× a Starter; **Enterprise (£396/mo)** earns ~4×.
- As customers need more leads/areas they **upgrade** — ARPU naturally rises over time (expansion revenue).
- Even a conservative mix makes blended ARPU £150/mo; pushing more Pro/Enterprise lifts it toward £200+/mo.

## Cash & profitability
- £25,000 raised; covers marketing/sales for roughly months 1-3 (~100 customers).
- From month 3 the business is **strongly cash-positive** (£22k+/mo revenue) — growth thereafter funded by revenue + the ~£0-CAC affiliate engine.
- **Break-even: month 3.** Payback per customer: ~£150 CAC vs ~£150-£300/mo customer value → **under 1 month**.

## The affiliate/staff engine (key to the curve)
- Staff, agents & customers refer businesses they know → earn **£25/sign-up** + reward wheel up to £1,000.
- Referred customers get a 14-day free trial — warm, pre-qualified.
- This is the **low-CAC scaling lever** that makes 600-1,000 customers in year 1 realistic.

# 3-Year Outlook

> For a £25k seed raise, the **12-month model above is the core**. This 3-year view shows the ambition and the path to a large, profitable company. It is **aspirational** — built on the affiliate engine scaling + revenue reinvestment, and it's what a follow-on (Series A) round would be based on.

## Assumptions
- Year 1 ends at **1,000 paying customers** (the 12-month model).
- Year 2-3 growth driven by: the affiliate/staff engine (~£0 CAC), expanding marketing budget from revenue, upsells (Starter → Pro → Enterprise), and 2-3 new verticals.
- Blended ARPU rises toward **£180/mo** by Y3 as the mix shifts to Pro/Enterprise.
- Print & Post stays at ~2× subscription (roughly doubling revenue per customer).
- Gross margin ~60-70% (subscription is high-margin; Print & Post ~40-50%).

## What investors actually want to see
1. **A credible Year 1** — the 12-month model (customers, ARPU, CAC, payback). This is what's scrutinised.
2. **Clear unit economics** — £150 ARPU, ~£150 CAC, payback <1 month.
3. **A believable scaling story** — the affiliate/staff engine + revenue reinvestment (not just "the money will make it happen").
4. **The 3-year destination** — shows you're thinking like a company worth £100M, not a side project.

## Follow-on round
- 3-year view positions a **Series A at 2-3,000 customers (~£8M/yr revenue)** — the natural next raise once the £25k seed proves the model.
