# SEIS Advance Assurance — Application Summary (9amLeads)

> Status: rules verified against gov.uk / HMRC manuals 3 Sep 2026. This is the content to paste/adapt into HMRC's **"Apply for Venture Capital Schemes (VCS) advance assurance"** digital form — NOT form SEIS1 (that is filed *after* shares are issued).
> Apply: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/venture-capital-schemes-apply-for-advance-assurance

## Why it matters
Advance assurance is a free, non-binding HMRC statement that the **proposed share issue** is likely to qualify for SEIS. Investors get:
- **50% income-tax relief** (up to £200k invested per person per tax year → up to £100k relief)
- **CGT reinvestment relief** (50% of the amount subscribed, max £100k of gains exempt)
- **CGT-free disposal** after 3 years · **loss relief** if it fails

Having AA **before** talking to angels is the single biggest close-rate lever for a £25k raise.

## After AA is granted
1. Issue shares → file **SEIS1** (compliance statement) with HMRC within 2 years of the issue date (online, via the same tax.service.gov.uk service).
2. HMRC issues **SEIS2/SEIS3** authorisation/certificates → investor claims relief via their SA return.
3. Keep evidence: bank statement of the £25k in, board minutes for the allotment, share certificates, latest accounts.
