## Where to send it (UK, £25k seed fits)
**Angel networks / groups**
- UK Business Angels Association (UKBAA) — broker introductions
- London Business Angels (LBA) — min tickets £10k+
- ANGLE (angel groups), Angel Investment Network (AIN) — direct pitch
- USK — Ulster Society of Kilted Angels (UK-wide)
- Innovate UK "Investors Partnership"

**Equity crowdfunding (good for a story like this)**
- Seedrs / Crowdcube — £25k is a standard first raise; crowd doubles as marketing

**Accelerators / small funding + support**
- NatWest Entrepreneur Accelerator · Barclays Eagle Lab · Tech Nation · StartUp Loans (up to £25k loan, not equity)

**Trade-adjacent investors (warmest fit)**
- Removal industry: BAR (British Association of Removers), OMA (OmniMovers), trade-show contacts
- Property/probate: local law society, estate agency owners — operators who'd "get it"
