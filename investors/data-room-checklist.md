# Data Room Checklist — 9amLeads

> What to have ready before any investor call or after they say "interested". Grouped by stage. Keep the data room in a private share (Google Drive/Dropbox folder named per investor or "9amLeads Data Room"), versioned and dated.

## 1. Ready now (send on request)
- [ ] **One-pager PDF** (`one-pager.md` → PDF, with the corrected funnel/numbers from `deck-critique.md`)
- [ ] **Pitch deck** (10 slides; from `pitch-deck-outline.md`)
- [ ] **12-month financial model** (`financial-model.md` → PDF/Excel; Excel with visible formulas is better than PDF for angels)
- [ ] Cap-table one-liner: £25k for 10% (post £250k), founder 90%, ordinary shares
- [ ] **Live demo** — dashboard (today's leads for a real account) + one real posted Stannp order + one verified lead record
- [ ] Real screenshots: dashboard, a delivered lead with full address, sent-mail order, delivery log

## 2. Company & legal
- [ ] Companies House: incorporation certificate, current appointments, registered address (confirm entity name — check what's on the deck matches)
- [ ] Memorandum & Articles of Association (Model Articles + any special provisions)
- [ ] PSC register (100% founder until the round)
- [ ] Board minutes for the proposed allotment (post-AA)
- [ ] Any previous share issues, option grants, or loans to/from the founder (must be clean or disclosed)

## 3. Tax / SEIS pack (see `seis-advance-assurance-summary.md`)
- [ ] **SEIS Advance Assurance submitted** + HMRC reference (do first)
- [ ] Post-issue: SEIS1 filed ≤2 years after issue; SEIS2/3 certificates for investors
- [ ] Confirmation the company passes the age test (trade carried on <3 years at share issue) or pivot to EIS
- [ ] Latest accounts / management accounts (gross assets under £350k for SEIS)

## 4. Product & ops proof
- [ ] Live dashboard access for the demo (a real customer account, or clean demo data)
- [ ] Delivery log: daily deliveries running for all 5 verticals (last 30 days)
- [ ] Verification evidence: sample lead with source URL + enrichment (door/street/postcode)
- [ ] Print & Post: real order screenshots + a physical sample letter/flyer to hand over
- [ ] Uptime/health: uptime monitor screenshot (auto-heal, backups working)
- [ ] 3–5 customer quotes or case studies (one per vertical ideally)

## 5. Marketing & funnel numbers (the gaps the critique flags)
- [ ] Funnel: total accounts created → current free trials → paying → MRR → revenue last 3 months
- [ ] Trial → paid conversion rate to date + cohort view if any
- [ ] Churn / retention of the 8 firms
- [ ] Affiliate programme status: # affiliates, # referrals generated, CAC by channel
- [ ] Lead-scraper dependency & resilience note (multi-source per vertical)

## 6. Financials & diligence
- [ ] Bank statements (last 3–6 months) + revenue by source (subscriptions vs print)
- [ ] Costs run-rate (hosting, Brevo, Stannp, tools, founder salary or drawings)
- [ ] Unit economics: ARPU, gross margin per stream, CAC, payback — one consistent set (see critique)
- [ ] Any Start Up Loan/grant in place or planned (disclose; it sits above the cap table)

## 7. Post-interest (before money)
- [ ] Short-form SEIS subscription & investment agreement (solicitor — budget a few hundred £)
- [ ] Share certificates + updated PSC/Companies House filings after allotment
- [ ] Investor questionnaire (HMRC) & proof the investor is unconnected and <30%
- [ ] Bank account details for the £25,000 (company account — never a personal account)

## Suggested folder structure
```
9amLeads Data Room/
├─ 00_Overview (one-pager, deck, model, cap table)
├─ 01_Legal (cert, articles, minutes, PSC)
├─ 02_Tax_SEIS (AA submission, SEIS1/2/3, eligibility proof)
├─ 03_Product_Demo (screenshots, delivery logs, sample mail, customer quotes)
├─ 04_Marketing_Funnel (funnel, conversion, churn, affiliate stats)
└─ 05_Financials (statements, run-rate, unit economics)
```
