Willow & Sage Solicitors
Probate
The Challenge

As a growing high-street firm in Bristol, Willow & Sage had a thriving conveyancing practice but their probate department was underperforming. They relied on existing clients referring family members and local will-writing partnerships — both of which were inconsistent and slow. Probate cases are high-value (typically £3,000–£8,000 per matter), but the firm was only picking up 2–3 new probate matters a month. They needed a way to reach executors who had just been granted probate, before their competitors did.

How 9am Leads Helped

The team subscribed to the Probate Leads stream. Every morning at 9am, they received newly granted probate applications from the official Gov.uk register in their catchment area. Because these leads are time-sensitive — executors typically start looking for a solicitor within days of the grant — the firm set up a simple process: the probate team called each lead by 10am, offering a free 15-minute consultation and a fixed-fee quote. They were often the first — and only — solicitor the executor spoke to.

Ascend Business Group
New Business
The Challenge

Ascend Business Group provides outsourced sales, marketing, and virtual CFO services to SMEs across the Midlands. Their growth depended entirely on their ability to consistently win new monthly retainers (£1,500–£5,000/month). They had a solid outreach team, but their lead sourcing was fragmented — LinkedIn Sales Navigator, purchased lists, referrals — and their conversion rate was stuck around 4%. They needed a stream of genuinely new businesses that had just incorporated and were likely to need external support from day one.

How 9am Leads Helped

Ascend subscribed to the New Business Alerts stream. Every morning at 9am, they received a list of newly incorporated companies in their target area, sourced directly from Companies House. Their SDRs prioritised businesses in high-growth sectors (tech, construction, professional services) and sent a personalised email and LinkedIn connection request within 90 minutes of the lead appearing. The timing was everything — new companies are actively looking for vendors in their first 30 days.
