4. Confirm their availability and timeline

I've attached a handy checklist that might help. No strings attached — just something I put together for my clients.

If you'd like to see how I compare against those criteria, I'm happy to share recent project photos and client testimonials.

Best,
[Your name]

Kind regards,
[Your name]

## 📠 Flyer Insert Templates

### Services Brochure (A5 Flyer)

**Front:** Bold headline — "Planning Approved? Let's Build." with your company name and logo.
**Inside:** List your services (extensions, loft conversions, renovations, etc.). Include 3 before/after project photos with brief captions.
**Back:** Client testimonials, your contact details, and a QR code linking to your portfolio website.
**Tip:** Print in colour on quality 170gsm+ paper. Hand-deliver in a weatherproof envelope.

### Special Offer Flyer

**Headline:** "Free consultation — worth £[value]"
**Body:** Offer a free site visit and written quote. Mention you're a local company with [X] years of experience.
**Call to action:** "Reply to this flyer or visit [website] to claim your free consultation."
**Tip:** Add a tear-off tab at the bottom with your phone/email/website.

### Case Study Flyer

**Headline:** "How we transformed [project type] in [area]"
**Body:** Tell the story of a recent project from initial enquiry to completion. Include photos, timeline, budget, and client quote.
**Bottom:** "Ready to start your own project? Call [phone] or visit [website]"
**Tip:** Keep it to one A4 page folded in thirds — it fits perfectly in a standard envelope.

## 🔄 Follow-Up Sequences

### Week 1: Initial Outreach
- Day 1: Post introduction letter + flyer + business card
- Day 2: Email introduction (use email template above)
- Day 3: Visit address in person — knock and introduce yourself
- Day 5: Post follow-up letter if no response

### Week 2: Build Relationship
- Day 8: Email tip/checklist (use email template above)
- Day 10: Second flyer drop with case study
- Day 12: Send SMS: "Hi [name], [your name] from [company] here. I popped by last week about your planning application. Just wanted to say I'm here if you need anything. My number is [phone]."

### Week 3-4: Final Touch
- Week 3: Final email nudge
- Week 4: Final flyer drop with special offer handwritten note
- After this: Move to quarterly check-in — send a postcard or Christmas card to stay top of mind

## ❓ Objection Handling

### "We're still waiting for planning approval"
**Response:** "No problem at all. I actually help clients prepare for approval so they're ready to start building the moment permission comes through. Would it help if I shared a few things you can be doing in the meantime?"
**Follow up:** Send your checklist of pre-approval preparation steps.

### "We're comparing quotes from a few builders"
**Response:** "That's sensible. I'd encourage you to look beyond the headline price — check the scope of work, materials specified, and timeline commitments. I've put together a comparison guide that might help. No pressure to choose today."
**Follow up:** Email your "Choosing the Right Builder" guide.

### "The budget isn't confirmed yet"
**Response:** "I completely understand. Many of my clients start with a free consultation where I walk through typical costs for a project like yours. There's absolutely no obligation. Would [day/time] work for a quick 15-minute call?"
**Follow up:** Send your free consultation offer flyer.

## ⭐ Best Practices

### Speed is everything
Planning applications are public and visible. Other builders are looking at the same leads. Be first with your letter and flyer — aim for same-day delivery.

### Personalisation wins
Reference the specific application — mention the address, the type of work proposed (extension, conversion, new build). Generic letters get binned. Personalised ones get read.

### Physical beats digital
An email is forgotten in seconds. A brochure on the kitchen table gets read over breakfast. Hand-deliver your materials for the best response rate.

### Follow up, don't give up
Most people don't respond to the first letter. Plan a 3-touch sequence (letter, visit, follow-up letter) over 2 weeks. Persistence pays.

## ⏰ Recommended Timing

### Best time to send letters
Monday or Tuesday — arrives mid-week when people are settled. Avoid Friday (ignored over weekend) and Monday morning (busy).

### Best time to visit in person
Late afternoon (4-6pm) or Saturday morning. People are home and more relaxed. Avoid lunchtime (12-2pm) and evening (after 7pm).

### Email timing
Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am. Open rates are highest mid-week mid-morning.
