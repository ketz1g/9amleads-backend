## 📧 Email Templates (12-Campaign)

[Your name]

### 2. Follow-up — Your property at [address]

Hi [name],

I dropped a brochure through your door last week about your property at [address]. I wanted to make sure it arrived and to see if you had any questions.

I know selling a home can feel overwhelming. I help sellers in [area] navigate the process from start to finish. Would you be open to a quick chat?

I'm happy to pop round at a time that suits you.

Best,
[Your name]

### 3. Tip — 3 things every seller should know

Hi [name],

I hope the sale of your property at [address] is going well. While you're in the process, here are three things every seller should consider:

1. First impressions matter — kerb appeal can add 5-10% to your final price
2. Getting the pricing right from day one attracts serious buyers faster
3. Having a clear timeline reduces stress and helps you plan your next move

I've put together a simple checklist that might help. No strings attached — just something I share with my clients.

If you'd like to chat about how I can help, I'm here.

Best,
[Your name]

### 4. Final nudge — Still here if you need me

Hi [name],

Just checking in one last time regarding your property at [address]. If the timing isn't right to make a change, I completely understand.

When you're ready, my details are below. I'll also pop another flyer through your door next week with an updated market insight for your area.

Wishing you all the best with your sale.

Kind regards,
[Your name]

## 📠 Flyer Insert Templates

### Property Services Brochure (A5 Flyer)

**Front:** Bold headline "Selling in [area]? Let's Talk." with your photo and company logo.
**Inside:** List your services (sales, lettings, valuations, etc.), recent sold prices, and 3 success stories.
**Back:** Client testimonials, contact details, QR code to your listings.
**Tip:** Print on quality paper. Hand-deliver in weatherproof envelope.

### Market Insight Flyer

**Headline:** "What's happening in [area]'s property market?"
**Body:** Show 3-4 local sold prices, average time to sell, current demand levels.
**Bottom:** "Want a free valuation? Call [phone] or visit [website]"
**Tip:** Update monthly and keep a stack in your car for impromptu drops.

### Homeowner Checklist Flyer

**Headline:** "10 Steps to a Stress-Free Sale"
**Body:** Numbered checklist from instruction to completion with your logo.
**Bottom:** "I help [area] homeowners sell faster. Let's talk."
**Tip:** Useful content gets kept — this flyer stays on the fridge.

## 🔄 Follow-Up Sequences

### Week 1: Initial Outreach
- Day 1: Post introduction letter + brochure + business card
- Day 2: Email introduction (use email template above)
- Day 3: Visit property in person — knock and introduce yourself
- Day 5: Post follow-up letter if no response

### Week 2: Build Relationship
- Day 8: Email market insight tip
- Day 10: Second flyer drop with market update
- Day 12: Send SMS: "Hi [name], [your name] from [company] here. I popped by about your property. Happy to help anytime — [phone]"

### Week 3-4: Final Touch
- Week 3: Final email nudge
- Week 4: Final flyer drop with handwritten note
- After: Quarterly check-in — send market updates to stay top of mind

## ❓ Objection Handling

### "I'm happy with my current agent"
**Response:** "That's great to hear. If things change or you'd like a second opinion on your property's value, I'm always happy to provide a free, no-obligation market appraisal. My service is built around a more personal approach."
**Follow up:** Send your client testimonials brochure.

### "I'm not sure about the timing"
**Response:** "Spring and autumn are traditionally the strongest selling seasons, but properties in [area] are selling year-round. I can show you recent comparable sales and help you decide what's right for your situation."
**Follow up:** Email your local market data flyer.

### "I need to discuss with my partner"
**Response:** "Of course. I've put together a simple information pack that explains everything — commission, marketing plan, timeline. Would it help if I posted a printed copy for you both to review together?"
**Follow up:** Post printed info pack with both their names on it.

## ⭐ Best Practices

### Speed matters
Properties go SSTC every day. Be first with your letter and flyer — aim for same-day delivery when a new listing appears in your area.

### Personalise everything
Reference the specific property address, the estate agent's description, and the asking price. Generic letters get ignored.

### Physical beats digital
A brochure on the kitchen table gets read. Hand-deliver your materials — it shows you're local and committed.

### Follow up systematically
Most people don't respond to the first letter. Plan a 3-touch sequence over 2 weeks.

---

## ⏰ Recommended Timing

### Best time to send letters
Tuesday or Wednesday — arrives mid-week when people are settled. Avoid Fridays and Mondays.

### Best time to visit in person
Late afternoon (4-6pm) or Saturday morning. Avoid lunchtime and after 7pm.

### Email timing
Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am. Highest open rates mid-week mid-morning.
