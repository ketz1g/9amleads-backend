# 🏢 New Business Success Kit

**10-email campaign · 3 letter templates · 2 flyer inserts · 3 follow-up sequences**

Here's exactly how successful agencies and consultants convert new business leads.

> ⚠️ **Important:** 9amLeads provides **addresses only** — we do not supply email addresses or phone numbers. You source those yourself. The most effective approach is posting a flyer + introduction letter either in person or via Royal Mail. In-person drops work best when you represent yourself professionally — sign-written company vehicle, branded uniform or smart attire, quality flyers, and a clear pitch.

## 📧 Email Templates (10-Campaign)

### 1. Congratulations on your new company registration

Hi [name],

Congratulations on registering [company name] with Companies House. Starting a new business is an exciting step, and I wanted to be one of the first to welcome you.

I'm [your name] from [company], and I help new businesses in [area] with [service — accounting, marketing, IT, consultancy, etc.]. Many of my clients started exactly where you are now.

I've put together a welcome pack for newly registered companies — it includes a guide to the first steps every new business should take. I'll also post a printed copy to your registered address this week.

If you'd find it helpful to have a no-obligation chat about how we might work together, I'd love to hear from you.

Best regards,
[Your name]

### 2. Follow-up — Your welcome pack for [company name]

Hi [name],

I hope you received the welcome pack I posted for [company name]. I wanted to check it arrived and to see how your first few weeks of trading are going.

If you'd like any help with [specific service], I'd be happy to have a brief call. There's no obligation — I simply want to introduce myself as a local resource for new businesses.

Best,
[Your name]

### 3. Tip — 5 things every new business should do in month one

Hi [name],

Running a new business is busy — I get it. Here are five things that will set you up for success in your first month:

1. Set up a separate business bank account
2. Register for VAT if your turnover will exceed £90,000
3. Get your accounting software in place
4. Set up a basic website with your business details
5. Register with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you handle personal data

I've put together a more detailed checklist. If you'd like a copy, just reply and I'll send it over.

Best,
[Your name]

### 4. Final nudge — Still here to help [company name]

Hi [name],

Just checking in one last time regarding [company name]. If the timing isn't right to bring in support yet, I completely understand.

When you're ready, my details are below. I'll also pop another information pack in the post next month with some useful resources for growing businesses.

Wishing you every success with your new venture.

Kind regards,
[Your name]

## 📠 Flyer Insert Templates

### New Business Services Flyer

**Front:** Bold "Congratulations on your new business!" with your company logo.
**Inside:** List your services tailored to new/small businesses. Include pricing options or package deals.
**Back:** Client testimonials, your qualifications, contact details.
**Tip:** Keep the tone celebratory and supportive — not salesy.

### Startup Checklist Flyer

**Headline:** "Your First 90 Days in Business — A Checklist"
**Body:** Actionable checklist across legal, financial, marketing, and operations.
**Bottom:** "Need help checking things off? Let's talk."
**Tip:** This is a high-value resource — businesses keep it pinned to their noticeboard.

### Services Overview (A4 Tri-Fold)

**Panel 1:** Your story — why you help new businesses.
**Panel 2:** Your services with clear pricing or packages.
**Panel 3:** Case study — how you helped a similar business succeed.
**Tip:** Use bullet points and clear headings. Busy founders skim-read.

## 🔄 Follow-Up Sequences

### Week 1: Initial Outreach
- Day 1: Post welcome letter + startup pack + business card
- Day 2: Email introduction (use email template above)
- Day 3: Visit registered address in person if local
- Day 5: Post follow-up letter

### Week 2: Nurture
- Day 8: Email tip/checklist
- Day 10: Second flyer drop with case study
- Day 12: Send SMS: "Hi [name], [your name] from [company]. I sent a welcome pack for [company name]. Happy to help at any stage — [phone]"

### Week 3-4: Final Touch
- Week 3: Final email nudge
- Week 4: Final flyer drop with special offer
- After: Quarterly check-in — send business tips or seasonal offers

## ❓ Objection Handling

### "We already have someone"
**Response:** "That's great to hear. If your needs ever change or you'd like a second opinion on anything — from pricing to service scope — I'd be happy to help. No pressure at all."
**Follow up:** Send your service comparison guide.

### "We're not ready yet / it's early days"
**Response:** "I completely understand — the first few months are about finding your feet. When you're ready to think about [service], I'd love to have a conversation. In the meantime, I'll send you a few resources that might be useful."
**Follow up:** Email your new business checklist.

### "We need to check our budget"
**Response:** "Of course. I offer flexible options designed for growing businesses. Would it help if I sent over a simple pricing breakdown with different package levels?"
**Follow up:** Post your pricing overview flyer.

## ⭐ Best Practices

### Be early
New companies register every day. Being the first to reach out positions you as proactive and attentive.

### Celebrate their launch
A new business registration is exciting. Your tone should be congratulatory and supportive, not transactional.

### Keep an eye on their website
Many new businesses launch their website weeks after registering. Check periodically and reference it in your follow-up.

### Build a relationship
Founders are busy. Don't expect an instant sale. Nurture the relationship over weeks and months.

---

## ⏰ Recommended Timing

### Best time to send letters
Within 48 hours of their company appearing on Companies House. Speed gives you first-mover advantage.

### Best time to visit
Late morning (10-12pm) or early afternoon (2-4pm). Registered offices may be staffed during business hours.

### Email timing
Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am. Business owners check email first thing.
