Everything you need to know about MovingLeadsDaily.

Moving leads are alerts we send you when a property in your territory goes SSTC (Sold Subject to Contract). This is the perfect moment for homeowners to book a removal company , they have exchanged contracts and need to arrange their move. We give you their contact details so you can be the first mover to call.

We monitor UK property portals for SSTC markers. When a property is listed as Sold Subject to Contract, our system captures the address, homeowner details, and property value. We then match it to your chosen territory and deliver it to your inbox the next morning at 9am.

Every lead delivered to your inbox is 100% exclusive , no other removal company contacts that same homeowner. You receive your daily limit at 9am. If more SSTC properties become available in your postcode area than your plan covers, those extras are offered to other removal companies. Upgrade your plan to capture more leads in your area before anyone else. The leads you receive are always yours alone.

Each lead includes the homeowner's name, property address, property value, and estimated move timeframe , sent to your email at 9am daily. You also get an opportunity score, an AI-drafted outreach message for email or phone, and CRM-ready export formats.
