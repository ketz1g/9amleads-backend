Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Probate Leads.

A probate grant is a legal document issued by the Probate Registry that confirms an executor's authority to administer a deceased person's estate. When someone dies, their executors must apply for probate before they can sell property, close bank accounts, or distribute assets. These grants are published on the official register, making them public record — and a powerful source of new business opportunities.
