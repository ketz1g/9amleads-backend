About Probate Leads

We help UK professionals find high-quality leads through daily data alerts from the official probate register.

What We Do

Probate Leads provides daily data alerts to help UK businesses find new clients. Our system monitors public data sources and delivers fresh leads directly to your inbox every morning — so you can be first to contact potential customers before your competitors even know they exist.

We track every probate grant published on the official UK Probate Register. Our technology captures grants within 24-48 hours of publication, filters them by your chosen postcodes or regions, and sends you a clean, actionable list every morning before 8 AM.

Whether you're a solicitor handling estate administration, an estate agent looking for property instructions, or a financial advisor offering inheritance tax planning — we give you the leads you need to grow your practice.
