🚚 Moving Leads

People who have listed their home for sale are actively planning a move. They need removal services, packing, and storage. Speed and professionalism win the business.

1
Research
Visit the property listing online to review property details and understand what the customer might need.
2
Tailor Quote
Create a specific quote based on property size, number of bedrooms, and estimated move distance.
3
Follow Up
Send a professional quote with a clear breakdown and a time-limited discount to encourage a quick decision.
🗺️

Target Your Area

Focus on properties within your operational radius. Local moves are your bread and butter — you can offer site visits easily and build a strong local reputation.

📸

Visual Your Service

Include photos of your team, your van, and past moves when presenting your quote. People trust what they can see.

⭐

Leverage Reviews

Share your Google or Trustpilot reviews in your communications. Social proof is one of the strongest conversion tools.

📋

Offer a Checklist

Provide a free moving-day checklist for your customers. It adds value and positions you as the helpful expert.

⚖️ Probate Leads

Estate executors need professional guidance to value and manage a deceased person's estate. Sensitivity and expertise are critical.

1
Understand
Research the estate and gather sufficient information about the property and its value to prepare a relevant proposal.
2
Proposal
Present a clear service package including probate valuation and any additional services you offer.
3
Guide
Provide a step-by-step guide to the probate process. Executors often feel overwhelmed and will appreciate clear, supportive advice.
📚

Educate Your Prospect

Executors are often unfamiliar with the probate process. A simple guide explaining the timeline and requirements will build trust and establish you as the authority.

💼

Offer a Full Service

Many estate agents and solicitors offer probate valuations as a gateway to securing the house sale instruction. Position yourself as a full-service partner.

🤝

Be Compassionate

This is a difficult time for the family. Professionalism with empathy goes a long way. Follow up thoughtfully and give them time to make decisions.

🏢 New Business Alerts

Newly registered companies are actively setting up operations. They need accountants, websites, insurance, office supplies, and more. You can reach them even without direct contact details.

1
Search Online
Search for the company name on Google. Many new businesses have a website, social media page, or directory listing with contact information.
2
Visit or Mail
Use the registered address to send a professional brochure, introductory letter, or leave a leaflet at their premises if local.
3
Connect
Send a LinkedIn connection request or follow their company page. Engage with their content to build familiarity before reaching out.
🌐

Check Their Website

Most limited companies will register a website soon after incorporation. Check the company name on Google — many will have at least a holding page with contact details listed.

📮

Leaflet Campaign

If the business is local, visit their registered address in person. A well-designed leaflet or introductory letter delivered by hand can be very effective. You can simply post it through their letterbox.

🔍

Use LinkedIn

Search for the company or director name on LinkedIn. Many directors list their profiles publicly. A polite connection request with a brief introduction can open the door.

📊

Track Your Outreach

Keep a simple log of which companies you have contacted, the method used, and the response. This helps you identify which approach works best for your area.

🔨 Planning Permission

Property owners applying for planning permission will soon need architects, builders, structural engineers, and interior designers. Get in early to secure the project.

1
Review Plans
Look up the application details on the council's planning portal to understand the scope of work.
2
Provide a relevant proposal or portfolio showing similar projects you have worked on, whether you are an architect, builder, or supplier.
3
Follow Up
Once permission is granted, contact the applicant promptly. There is often a window of opportunity immediately after approval.
📋

Council Portal Research

Each council publishes planning applications online with detailed plans, documents, and contact details. Use the reference number from your lead to pull up the full application.

🏗️

Showcase Your Work

Whether you are an architect, builder, or interior designer, having a portfolio of similar projects ready will help the applicant visualise what you can deliver for them.

⏱️

Timing Matters

Planning permission typically lasts 3 years. Contact applicants soon after approval is granted, as they are most likely to be planning their next steps during this period.

📋 Public Tenders

Government and public sector organisations publish contract opportunities. These are formal bidding processes that require careful preparation.

1
Review
Read the full tender document carefully. Understand the requirements, evaluation criteria, and submission deadline.
2
Prepare
Gather all necessary documentation including insurance certificates, quality accreditations, and case studies of similar contracts.
3
Submit
Complete the tender response thoroughly, addressing each evaluation criterion. Submit well before the deadline to avoid last-minute issues.
📂

Prepare a Tender Template

Create a standard tender response template with your company information, insurance details, and policies. This will save hours when new opportunities arise.

🎯

Quality Over Quantity

Focus on tenders where you have relevant experience. A focused, high-quality submission is far more likely to succeed than generic bids for everything available.

📅

Track Deadlines

Set calendar reminders for tender deadlines at least 3 days before the closing date. This gives you time to handle any last-minute document requests or technical issues.

💡 Universal Tips for All Lead Types

⚡

Speed Is Key

Contact prospects promptly. Being first to respond gives you a significant advantage over competitors who wait until later.

🎯

Personalise Everything

Reference their specific situation, property, or industry in your communications. Generic messages get ignored. Personalised approaches build trust and get responses.

📊

Track Your Results

Log every lead, every contact attempt, and every conversion on your dashboard. Review which lead types and areas deliver the best return and focus your efforts there.

🔄

Follow Up More Than Once

Most conversions happen after the second or third contact. A polite follow-up a few days later can turn a maybe into a yes.

📝

Keep Records

Maintain a simple spreadsheet of prospects you have contacted, the date, method used, and any response. This helps you refine your approach over time.

📈

Review and Refine

Check your dashboard regularly to see which lead types and postcode areas perform best. Adjust your filters and territories to maximise your return.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set my postcode territories?

Go to your dashboard and click "Edit Territories" in the My Territories section. You can search for postcode districts or type specific sectors (e.g., "EN1 3", "SG13 7"). Your plan includes a set number of sectors - free trial includes 5. Need more? Add extra sectors for £25 one-time each from your subscription settings.

How fresh are the leads?

Leads are scraped daily at 4am from official UK data sources including Companies House, the Planning Portal, and Contracts Finder. You receive the most recent opportunities available.

What if I don't have contact details?

Some lead types do not include direct contact information. For new business alerts, check the company's website or search for them on LinkedIn. You can also send a leaflet or letter to the registered address listed in your lead. For moving leads, the property address is provided for your reference.

Can I change my lead type or postcode areas?

Yes. Go to Settings in your dashboard to update your lead types and postcode territories. Changes take effect for the next day's delivery.

How do I set up filters?

Go to Settings in your dashboard and click "Edit Filters" under Lead Filters. You can set criteria for each lead type individually, such as minimum bedrooms for moving leads or industry categories for new business alerts.
