Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Moving Leads Daily

Is there a free trial?

Yes! Moving Leads Daily is free during our launch period. No card required to start receiving leads.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, no minimum commitment. Cancel from your dashboard with one click.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major debit/credit cards via Stripe, plus PayPal. All payments are processed securely.

How many leads will I get?

Pro members get unlimited leads. The number depends on your area and competition. Most clients receive 20-50+ leads per week.

Can I try before paying?

Yes! Start with our free Starter plan. Upgrade to Pro anytime when you're ready for more leads.

Is this connected to other services?

Moving Leads Daily works alongside The Moving School and other removal industry services. We're building integrated solutions.
