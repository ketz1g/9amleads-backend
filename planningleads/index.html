Six Trades That Profit from Planning Leads

Every approved planning application creates work for specific trades. Here is why each one needs to be first to contact the applicant.

Builders

Every approved extension or new build needs a main contractor. Planning applications tell you exactly who is building what and where before they have chosen their builder. Be the first to quote and you win more work.

Architects

Homeowners need architectural drawings and design statements before they can submit planning applications. Spot applications that need professional design input and offer your services early in the process.

Electricians

New builds, extensions, and loft conversions all require full electrical rewires and installations. Planning data reveals projects before they break ground, giving you weeks of advantage over other electricians.

Plumbers

Every extension adds bathrooms or kitchens. Every new build needs full heating and plumbing systems. Planning applications tell you exactly where the work will happen and at what scale.

Roofers

Extensions, loft conversions, and new builds all need professional roofing. Planning applications reveal the project scope and estimated build cost before construction starts. Quote early, win the contract.

Landscapers

Approved developments often require landscaping as a condition of planning. Homeowners with new extensions need gardens redesigned and patios installed. Spot these requirements in the planning documents and be the landscaper they call first.
